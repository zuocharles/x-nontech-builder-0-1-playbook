# X Non-Tech Builder 0→1 Playbook

A playbook for non-technical builders who want to establish authority on X (Twitter) through shipping AI projects and documenting their journey.

## How This Skill Works

When you invoke this skill, here's what happens:

1. **Read Your Profile**: I'll ask for your X handle and analyze your current positioning, bio, pinned tweet, recent posts, and engagement patterns. This grounds everything in your actual presence.

2. **Understand Your Why**: We'll clarify your purpose on X (lead gen, authority, community building, content distribution) so every decision serves that goal.

3. **Provide Targeted Guidance**: Based on what you ask for—whether it's evaluating a draft post, establishing your voice, planning engagement, or optimizing your profile—I'll give you actionable feedback that fits your unique positioning.

4. **Help You Execute**: From content pillars to reply timing to deplatforming strategy, I'll guide you through every stage of building your X presence.

## What You Can Use This For

- **Profile Optimization**: Get your bio, pinned tweet, banner, and profile picture working together to attract your target audience
- **Voice & Positioning**: Define who you help, what you build, and how to communicate it authentically
- **Post Evaluation**: Ask "Should I post this?" and I'll check it against your positioning and the "From vs About" test
- **Content Strategy**: Build a sustainable content mix with clear pillars and weekly cadence
- **Engagement Playbook**: Learn exactly who to reply to, when, and with what
- **Ship Documentation**: Turn your projects into compelling posts that drive growth

## Key Principles

- **From vs About**: Every post must come from personal experience. You are the protagonist, not a commentator.
- **Content Pillars**: Focus on ship posts, learning posts, workflow teardowns, and extensions (not listicles or frameworks).
- **Algorithm-Aware**: Understand what X rewards (dwell time, replies from non-followers, images/demos) and build for it.
- **Strategic Engagement**: Spend most energy on peers your size (<10K), reply strategically to established builders (10K-100K), and study the big accounts (>100K) for format inspiration.
- **Owned Channels**: Move your audience to newsletter, Substack, or community—don't rely on X alone.

## Quick Reference: Content Mix (GEO Method)

- **60% Growth**: Ship posts, build-in-public, lessons learned (high engagement potential)
- **30% Engagement**: Replies to peers and established builders (relationship and visibility building)
- **10% Offer**: CTAs to newsletter, artifact links, community invites (conversion)

## Quick Reference: Weekly Cadence

- **Monday**: Reply to 2 posts from peers your size (<10K followers)
- **Wednesday**: Reply to 3 ship posts from established builders (10K-100K)
- **Friday**: Ship something new + post about it
- **Weekend**: Engage casually, quote tweet interesting ideas

## Quick Reference: Post Evaluation Checklist

Ask yourself these before posting:

- [ ] Does it pass the "From vs About" test? (Personal experience, not external commentary)
- [ ] Am I the protagonist of this post?
- [ ] Does it fit one of my 4 content pillars?
- [ ] Would my target audience stop scrolling for this?
- [ ] Does it create dwell time? (image, demo, or specific result)
- [ ] Is the hook in the first line?
- [ ] Is there a clear next action? (reply prompt, CTA, or link)
