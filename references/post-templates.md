# Post Templates: High-Performing Structures

Use these templates as starting points. Replace the bracketed sections with your actual experience.

## Ship Post Templates

### Template 1: Simple Ship
```
I built [what] today/this week.

[One specific detail or number]

[Optional: What happens next or why it matters]
```

**Example**:
"I built an AI API wrapper today. Lets me pipe Claude directly into my CRM.

Saved 8 hours of manual data cleanup every week.

Already working on packaging this as a product."

**Why it works**: Direct, specific, shows outcome in concrete terms.

---

### Template 2: Problem → Solution → Outcome
```
I was [problem] [context].

So I built [solution].

[Specific outcome/metric].
```

**Example**:
"I was getting 200+ Slack messages a day and missing the important ones.

So I built a Claude bot that summarizes threads by priority.

Now I process Slack in 12 minutes instead of 90."

**Why it works**: Problem-Solution-Outcome is a proven narrative arc.

---

### Template 3: Before/After
```
Before: [old way, with metric]

After: [new way with AI, with metric]

What changed: [one key insight about the change]
```

**Example**:
"Before: 45 minutes per morning responding to customer emails.

After: 15 minutes (Claude drafts replies, I edit and send).

What changed: I went from writing to editing. Quality is actually better because I'm not rushed."

**Why it works**: Before/after creates immediate context and shows clear value.

---

### Template 4: Ship + Learnings
```
Shipped [what]. [Quick outcome].

Top 3 things I got wrong:
1. [Mistake 1]
2. [Mistake 2]
3. [Mistake 3]

Next iteration: [One specific thing you'll change]
```

**Example**:
"Launched my AI writing course. 12 signups in 48 hours.

Top 3 things I got wrong:
1. Overestimated demand for 'advanced' features
2. Underestimated how much people want examples
3. Priced it wrong (too low initially, should've done higher)

Next iteration: Doubling the price and adding 15 real-world case studies."

**Why it works**: Vulnerability + learning + action plan builds trust.

## Learning / Failure Post Templates

### Template 1: Failure + Root Cause
```
[Specific failure with metric]

What I got wrong: [root cause analysis]

If I did it again: [specific change]
```

**Example**:
"Spent 2 weeks building an AI tool for Instagram analytics. 0 sales.

What I got wrong: Built it for a problem I invented, not one people had. Skipped talking to potential customers.

If I did it again: 10 customer interviews before I write any code."

**Why it works**: Teaches by showing a real mistake and the fix.

---

### Template 2: Expectation vs Reality
```
I thought [expectation, with reasoning]

Turns out [reality]

The difference: [what you learned]
```

**Example**:
"I thought GPT-4 would dominate Claude for code generation because it was newer.

Turns out Claude actually beat it on 70% of my test cases.

The difference: Claude's context window + instruction-following > raw model size."

**Why it works**: Surprises people and changes their mental model.

---

### Template 3: Iteration Story
```
Version 1: [What you built]

Problem: [What didn't work]

Version 2: [What you changed]

Result: [New outcome]
```

**Example**:
"Version 1: My AI course positioned as 'How to Build AI Products'

Problem: Attracted experienced devs, not my actual audience. 3 sales.

Version 2: Repositioned as 'How to Make Money with AI (No Coding)' for solopreneurs

Result: 47 sales. 10x better because positioning changed who saw it."

**Why it works**: Shows iteration is normal and powerful.

## Workflow Teardown Templates

### Template 1: Step-by-Step Process
```
Here's my [process name] in [time]:

1. [Step 1] ([time])
2. [Step 2] ([time])
3. [Step 3] ([time])
4. [Step 4] ([time])

Key insight: [one sentence about what matters]
```

**Example**:
"Here's how I go from raw idea to published post in 30 minutes:

1. Voice memo of raw thoughts (5 min)
2. Claude transcribes + bullet points (3 min)
3. I rewrite for my voice + add examples (15 min)
4. Find image + schedule (5 min)
5. Publish

Key insight: The AI does the capture and structure. The human does the voice. That's where the value is."

**Why it works**: Specific time breakdown builds credibility. Key insight teaches.

---

### Template 2: Tool Comparison From Experience
```
Tested [tool 1], [tool 2], and [tool 3] for [task]

Thought [tool X] would win.

Winner: [tool Y] because [specific reason from your test]

Trade-offs:
- [Tool 1]: [Pro/con]
- [Tool 2]: [Pro/con]
- [Tool 3]: [Pro/con]
```

**Example**:
"Tested Claude, GPT-4, and Gemini for customer email analysis.

Thought GPT-4 would win.

Winner: Claude because it extracted intent + sentiment 40% more accurately on my specific use case.

Trade-offs:
- Claude: Slower, most accurate
- GPT-4: Faster, decent accuracy
- Gemini: Cheapest, most inconsistent"

**Why it works**: You tested it yourself. Readers trust firsthand experience.

## Extension / Quote Tweet Templates

### Template 1: Agree + Add
```
[Quote tweet]

This. I'd add: [your experience] 

My data: [one specific result from applying the idea]
```

**Example**:
"[Quote: 'The best marketing is having a product that's so good people want to tell their friends']

This. I'd add: But you have to make telling people EASY.

My data: 40% of my users came from referrals only after I added a one-click share button. Before that? Less than 5%."

**Why it works**: Validates the original idea + adds your proof.

---

### Template 2: Productive Disagreement
```
[Quote tweet]

I'd push back on [specific claim]. Here's why:

[Your experience that contradicts or nuances the claim]
```

**Example**:
"[Quote: 'AI will replace all content writers']

I'd push back. AI is a commodity if you use it for commodity outputs. But layered with real experience + unique perspective? That's defensible.

I've seen people charge 10x more by treating AI as a tool, not a replacement."

**Why it works**: Respectful, nuanced, grounded in your experience.

---

### Template 3: Specific Example
```
[Quote tweet]

Exactly. Example from my work:

[Specific instance where this principle applied to you]
```

**Example**:
"[Quote: 'Your network is your net worth']

Exactly. Example from my work:

Got my first 3 clients from replying thoughtfully to their posts on X. Spent 30 min/day for a month. That 30 min/day is now $30k/month in revenue because those relationships compounded."

**Why it works**: Concrete example makes abstract idea tangible.

## Direct CTA Templates (5% of content)

### Template 1: Newsletter CTA
```
[Value prop in one sentence]

[Why this matters to them]

[CTA with link]
```

**Example**:
"I ship a weekly breakdown of what worked on X and what didn't (with the data).

If you're building an audience on X, this is how you cut through the noise.

Subscribe: [link] (Free, 2 min read)"

**Why it works**: Clear value + specificity + low friction (free).

---

### Template 2: Artifact CTA
```
Built [what]. 

[Quick benefit]

It's [price/free].

Link: [artifact link]
```

**Example**:

12 templates, from ship posts to replies to engagement starters. Copy, paste, fill in your details.

It's free.

[Gumroad link]"

**Why it works**: Low pressure, high value, easy to try.

---

### Template 3: Community CTA
```

We're currently [size/stage].

Join: [link]
```

**Example**:
"Building a community of non-technical founders shipping AI products.

We're 200 people deep. Shipping updates, feedback on drafts, accountability for launches.

**Why it works**: Community feels like you're part of something bigger.

## General Structure Rules

**The Hook** (first sentence): This determines whether people keep reading. Make it specific and surprising.

**The Body**: Deliver on the hook's promise with specific details, not generic advice.

**The CTA** (implicit or explicit): Tell people what to do next. Reply, click, save, subscribe, share.

**The Proof**: Use numbers, examples, or outcomes whenever possible.

---

## Template Usage Tips

1. Don't copy templates word-for-word. Use them as structure, not content.
2. Your voice matters more than the template. Break the template if it helps your voice shine.
3. Test different templates and see which gets more engagement.
4. Track what works. Update your own templates based on your data.

The best template is the one that works for YOUR audience, YOUR voice, and YOUR goals.
