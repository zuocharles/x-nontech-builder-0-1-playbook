# Engagement Playbook: Build Real Relationships on X

Strategic engagement is how you build followers who actually care. This playbook gives you the exact framework to decide who to engage with and how.

## The Three Engagement Buckets

Not all accounts are equal. Spend your engagement energy strategically based on account size.

### Peers (< 10K followers)
**Who**: Builders in your space with less than 10K followers. People you can actually have a relationship with.

**Engagement Strategy**: Reply daily. Build real relationships. Show up for their posts.

**Why**: 
- They remember who you are
- They reply back (high engagement velocity)
- These relationships compound into collabs, guest posts, referrals
- Early audience members often become advocates

**How to Find Peers**:
- Search hashtags related to your niche (#buildinpublic, #aiship, etc.)
- Look at replies to bigger accounts' posts — often peers lurking there
- Check who follows people in your space
- Look at quote tweets on big posts

**How Often**: At minimum 5 replies per week. Ideal: 1 reply daily.

---

### Established Builders (10K-100K followers)
**Who**: People with real presence and authority in your space. Proven audience.

**Engagement Strategy**: Reply to ship posts 2-3x per week. Be selective. Add genuine value.

**Why**:
- Their followers see your reply
- Good chance of profile clicks
- Visibility boost for your profile
- People notice thoughtful contributions

**How to Find Established Builders**:
- Follow people in your industry/niche
- Set up alerts for keywords (if using TweetDeck)
- Look at who talks about your tools or audience
- Subscribe to their content

**How Often**: 2-3 ship post replies per week. Not every post—only ones where you have real value to add.

---

### Big Accounts / Influencers (> 100K followers)
**Who**: People with massive reach. Thought leaders. Celebrities of your space.

**Engagement Strategy**: Lurk, study, quote tweet selectively.

**Why**:
- They're overwhelmed with replies
- Your individual reply is noise
- But a great quote tweet can catch attention
- Use them as inspiration, not engagement targets

**How to Find Big Accounts**:
- The people everyone follows
- Media mentions
- People with 100K+ in your field

**How Often**: Quote tweet interesting insights 1-2x per month. Or don't — focus on peers and established builders first.

## The Daily Engagement Formula

Spending 30 minutes on X daily is more effective than 3 hours on Sunday.

### 5-Minute Morning Routine
1. Check X for 5 minutes
2. Find 1 post from a peer your size to reply to
3. Reply thoughtfully (specific, adds value, asks a question)
4. Move on

Repeat this daily, that's 5 replies to peers per week = relationship building.

### Weekly Engagement Breakdown (30-40 min/week)

**Monday** (10 min)
- Reply to 2 ship posts from peers your size (<10K)
- Focus on people who shipped something or had a learning

**Wednesday** (10 min)
- Reply to 3 ship posts from established builders (10K-100K)
- Look for technical or positioning insights you can contribute

**Friday** (10 min)
- Engage with engagement. Reply to replies to your posts.
- Like and quote tweet interesting takes from big accounts

**Weekend** (5-10 min)
- Casual scrolling
- No forced engagement
- Reply if something genuinely speaks to you

## The 30-Minute Reply Window (Critical)

**When a post goes live, the first 30 minutes are when your reply gets the most visibility.**

- Posts from 0-5 minutes get 10x more visibility than posts from 5-30 minutes
- Posts from 5-30 minutes get 10x more visibility than posts from 30-2 hours
- First 5 replies on a post get exponential boost

**Strategy**: 
- Check X 2-3x per day (morning, midday, evening)
- When you see a great post from a peer or established builder, reply within 30 minutes
- Don't stress about perfect replies—authentic and quick beats perfect and late

## How to Write a High-Value Reply

**Bad reply**: "Great post! Love this." → Adds no value. Gets ignored.

**Good reply**: Specific, adds value, shows you read it.

### Reply Structure

```
[Acknowledge what they said or asked]

[Add value: share data, ask specific question, offer perspective from your experience]

[Optional: Ask them a follow-up question]
```

### Example Replies

**Their post**: "Just launched my AI course. 47 sales in first week."

Bad reply: "Congrats! Amazing work. 🔥"

Good reply: "47 is solid for week 1. What's your refund rate looking like? I ask because I see a lot of AI courses with high interest but people bounce after day 1."

---

**Their post**: "I've been testing Claude vs GPT-4 for customer support."

Bad reply: "Interesting! Which did you prefer?"

Good reply: "How did you measure quality? I found Claude's context window helped with support since our conversations are usually 15+ messages. But GPT-4 was faster on simple requests."

---

**Their post**: "Built a workflow that saves me 20 hours/week."

Bad reply: "This is gold 🙏"

Good reply: "What was the biggest bottleneck you automated? I'm at 50% automation now and the last 50% seems to be edge cases that need human judgment."

---

### Reply Formula Breakdown

1. **Acknowledge**: Show you read and understood
2. **Add value**: Share data, ask specific question, offer experience
3. **Engage**: Ask a follow-up or invite them to dig deeper

This takes 2 minutes to write. That's enough.

## Strategy by Engagement Level

### If You're Just Starting (< 100 followers)
Focus entirely on peers your size. Build relationships. Quality > quantity.

- Daily: 1 thoughtful reply
- Weekly: 5-7 total replies
- Goal: Get known in your niche, not outside it

### If You're Building (100-1K followers)
Peers heavily, start adding established builders.

- Daily: 1 reply to a peer
- 2-3x per week: 1 reply to an established builder
- Weekly: 7-10 total replies
- Goal: Visibility + relationship

### If You Have Momentum (1K-10K followers)
Balance peers and established builders. Peers stay core.

- 3-4x per week: Replies to peers (maintain relationships)
- 2-3x per week: Replies to established builders (build visibility)
- Weekly: 10-15 total replies
- Goal: Traffic from established builders, loyalty from peers

### If You're Established (10K+ followers)
Your followers engage with you. Use replies to support community.

- Daily: Reply to replies to your posts
- 2-3x per week: Reply to peers (support the community)
- 1-2x per week: Reply to established builders (selective visibility play)
- Weekly: 10-20 replies, mostly to people supporting you

## Specific Engagement Playbooks by Goal

### Goal: Build Authority
Focus on established builders (10K-100K). Reply to their ship posts. Show expertise through replies.

- Monday: Reply to 1 established builder's technical post
- Wednesday: Reply to 1 established builder's positioning post
- Friday: Reply to 1 established builder's shipping post
- Plus: 2-3 replies to peers daily

### Goal: Grow Audience Fast
Focus on peers. Build deep relationships. Get advocates.

- Daily: 1-2 replies to peers (build relationships)
- 2x per week: 1 reply to an established builder (visibility)
- Goal: Peers become advocates and share your stuff

### Goal: Build Community
Mixed. Reply to many people. Make them feel seen.

- 5-10 replies to peers per week (make people feel included)
- 3-5 replies to established builders per week (invite them into your community)
- Reply to every reply to your posts (high engagement)

### Goal: Drive Traffic
Target established builders selectively. Quality replies that drive profile clicks.

- 2-3x per week: Reply to an established builder (pick posts with high engagement)
- High quality replies that showcase your expertise
- Include subtle CTA in bio ("Link in profile for [offer]")

## Tools for Finding Engagement Opportunities

### TweetDeck (Free)
- Create columns for keywords
- Get alerts for posts about your niche
- Set up columns for peers and established builders you follow

### Nitter or X Advanced Search
- Search: "from:@username" to find someone's recent posts
- Search keywords + date filters to find trending posts in your space

### Manual Scrolling (Still the Best)
- Spend 10 min/day actually scrolling
- You'll see patterns of who's posting what
- Intuition matters—reply when something resonates

## Red Flags to Avoid

**Don't engage with:**
- Toxic threads (people looking for fights)
- Spam or scams
- People not in your niche (wasted effort)
- Posts that only want agreement, not discussion

**Avoid saying:**
- "Great post!" (adds zero value)
- "I totally agree." (no insight)
- Emojis only (looks low effort)
- Irrelevant take (shows you didn't read)

## Measuring What Works

Track these for 2 weeks:

1. **Replies that get RT'd**: What did they say? What was the tone?
2. **Replies that get follow-ups**: Which ones sparked conversation?
3. **Replies that drive profile clicks**: (Check via analytics if available)

Pattern match. Double down on what works.

## The Long Game

Engagement isn't about going viral on one post. It's about:

1. Being known in your niche as someone worth following
2. Having people recognize your name and know what you do
3. Building real relationships that lead to collabs, partnerships, audience referrals
4. Creating visibility for your work

**That happens over months, not days.**

Spend 30 minutes per week on strategic engagement. Show up daily for your peers. It will compound.
