# Voice Examples: Building Your X Presence

This file contains example voices and tones for different post types. Use these as inspiration to develop your own authentic voice.

## Ship Posts (60% of your content)

**What they are**: Posts about something you actually built, launched, or shipped. These drive 20-175x follower impressions.

### Example 1: Simple Ship
"Built a no-code AI prompt library in Airtable today. 47 templates for writers. Took 4 hours. Already had someone ask to buy it. Might actually turn this into something."

**Why it works**: 
- Personal ("I built", "took 4 hours")
- Specific outcome (47 templates, someone asked to buy)
- Shows the journey, not just the result

### Example 2: Ship with Honest Numbers
"Launched my AI copywriting framework as a $29 Gumroad product 3 days ago. 12 sales, $348 revenue. Not earth-shattering, but it's real. Here's what I learned about positioning from those 3 customers."

**Why it works**:
- Vulnerable with real numbers
- Shows effort and authenticity
- Sets up learnings

### Example 3: Ship with Problem-Outcome
"I was drowning in email. Built a Claude API wrapper that summaries my inbox every morning. 5 minutes vs 45 minutes. Biggest win: I actually read them now instead of marking read."

**Why it works**:
- Opens with relatable pain
- Specific outcome (5 min vs 45 min)
- Emotional result ("actually read them")

## Honest Failure / Learning Posts (15% of your content)

**What they are**: Posts about what didn't work, lessons learned, or mistakes made. These build trust and authority.

### Example 1: Failed Launch
"Spent 2 weeks building an AI tool no one wanted. Crickets on launch day. Turns out I skipped customer research and built for a problem I invented. Expensive lesson. Here's what I'd do differently..."

**Why it works**:
- Specific failure (2 weeks, no demand)
- Root cause analysis (skipped research)
- Action item (here's what I'd do differently)

### Example 2: Pivot Story
"My first AI course was too technical. 3 sales in a month. Reframed it for non-technical founders. 47 sales the next month. The product didn't change. The positioning did."

**Why it works**:
- Shows iteration and learning
- Specific before/after (3 vs 47)
- Core insight (positioning > product)

### Example 3: Tool Comparison
"Tried Claude, GPT-4, and Gemini for my latest project. Thought GPT-4 would win. Claude actually gave better results for code generation. Here's what I tested and why."

**Why it works**:
- Direct comparison from experience
- Surprises (expected one thing, got another)
- Transparency about methodology

## Workflow Teardowns (10% of your content)

**What they are**: Posts showing exactly how you use AI to do something. Step-by-step, specific, actionable.

### Example 1: Morning Routine
"Here's my exact AI-powered morning routine (15 minutes):

1. Claude summarizes my inbox (via Zapier)
2. ChatGPT generates 3 post ideas based on trends
3. Gemini writes first draft of newsletter
4. I edit and ship

The key: I treat Claude as my editor, not my writer."

**Why it works**:
- Step-by-step format
- Specific tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini)
- Philosophy at the end (editor vs writer)

### Example 2: Content Creation
"My process for going from idea to shipped post in 30 minutes:

- Capture raw thoughts in voice memo (3 min)
- Claude transcribes + structures (3 min)
- I rewrite for my voice (15 min)
- Fact-check + image search (5 min)
- Ship it

The bottleneck is always the rewrite. I can't automate voice."

**Why it works**:
- Time breakdown
- Shows where AI adds value
- Identifies the human part

## Extension Posts (10% of your content)

**What they are**: Posts that respond to someone else's idea, adding your own experience or perspective.

### Example 1: Agreeing + Adding
"[Quote tweet from Naval about leverage]

This is true. I'd add: for technical people, shipping AI projects IS your leverage. I went from freelancer (hourly) to productized (30 clients/month) in 4 months because I automated my workflow with Claude."

**Why it works**:
- Agrees with core idea
- Adds personal data point
- Shows specific outcome

### Example 2: Productive Disagreement
"[Quote tweet about AI writing being a commodity]

I'd push back. AI is a commodity if you use it for commodity outputs (generic blog posts). But if you layer your experience + voice? That's defensible. I've seen people charge 10x more using this approach."

**Why it works**:
- Respectful disagreement
- Nuance (commodity vs defensible)
- Specific mechanism (experience + voice)

### Example 3: Building On Ideas
"[Quote tweet about founder burnout]

The real move: use AI to outsource the execution, not the thinking. I spend 80% of my time on strategy/vision and 20% on shipping (used to be opposite). Output doubled, stress halved."

**Why it works**:
- Acknowledges original idea
- Offers a specific framework (outsource execution)
- Shares outcome

## Community / Conversation Starters (5% of your content)

**What they are**: Posts that ask questions, invite discussion, or start conversations without a hard CTA.

### Example 1: Genuine Question
"Quick survey: When you ship an AI product, what kills you more—getting the first 10 customers or keeping them engaged?

Asking because I found the first 10 is harder than scaling."

**Why it works**:
- Real question you care about
- Shows you have skin in the game
- Invites replies

### Example 2: Hot Take
"Hot take: Most 'AI tools' are just prompt wrappers. The ones that win are the ones solving real workflow problems. Anyone building something that actually changes how someone works day-to-day?"

**Why it works**:
- Opinioned without being inflammatory
- Specific (workflow problems)
- Invites examples from others

## What to AVOID

### Bad: Generic Frameworks
❌ "Top 10 AI Tools for Productivity" — Caps at 0.5-0.8x followers. You're not the protagonist.

❌ "5 Steps to Build Your AI Startup" — Generic advice from nobody. Where's your experience?

✅ Better: "I tested 15 AI tools for my startup. Here's the 3 that actually stayed in my daily workflow (and why the other 12 didn't)."

### Bad: External Commentary Without Experience
❌ "OpenAI just announced GPT-4.5. Here's what it means." — You're a news reporter, not a builder.

✅ Better: "Tested GPT-4.5 on my customer support automation. 40% better context window means 30% fewer hallucinations in my use case."

### Bad: All Ship, No Humanity
❌ "Shipped v2 of my app. New features: auth, webhooks, analytics." — Where's the story? Why should anyone care?

✅ Better: "Shipped v2 of my app. Built auth because 40% of users were sharing accounts. Built webhooks because one customer asked and I realized 5 others wanted it too."

## Your Voice Development Exercise

1. Write 3 ship posts in YOUR voice (not these examples)
2. Post them
3. Notice which one gets the most engagement
4. That's the direction to go

Your authentic voice will outperform any template. These are starting points, not final answers.
